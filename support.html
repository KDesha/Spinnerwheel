Purchases and subscriptions

iPhone or iPad: Use Account → View plans → Restore purchases if a paid plan is missing. Manage or cancel an Apple subscription in App Store subscription settings.

Android or web: RevenueCat Billing and Stripe process web subscriptions. Use the secure update or manage your subscription link in your purchase confirmation, renewal, or billing email to change your payment method, view receipts, or cancel. If that email is missing, contact support from the email address on your Spines & Spins account.

Account deletion

Open your account menu and choose Delete account. The app will explain what is removed and ask you to type DELETE before anything is permanently deleted. Account deletion does not cancel billing; cancel an Apple subscription in App Store settings or a web subscription through the secure management link in your RevenueCat billing email first.

Community safety

Use Report beneath a chapter message to send it to the moderation queue. Use Block reader to immediately hide that person’s messages. For an urgent concern, email support with the club name and a short description; do not send passwords or payment details.

Book lookup

Try the complete title, author, or ISBN. Google Books is the primary catalog and Open Library fills some missing details. You can always enter a book manually if a catalog does not have it.
