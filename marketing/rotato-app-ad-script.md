# Spines & Spins — Rotato Hero Ad

## Creative direction

- **Length:** 60 seconds
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical master, with all important text and the phone kept inside a 4:5-safe center area
- **Story:** A real book-club problem becomes an app, then the app takes viewers through the entire club journey
- **Voice:** Founder speaking to another reader—warm, genuine, lightly playful, never “announcer-like”
- **Visual mood:** Dark forest, deep burgundy, midnight green, warm gold, floating dust and subtle magical light
- **Music:** Cinematic bookish wonder; begin intimate, lift at the first wheel spin, soften under chapter conversations, and resolve brightly at the logo

## Final voiceover

> My book club needed this app so badly, I built it.
>
> Choosing our next read always became an endless group-chat debate. So Spines & Spins gives the whole club one shared home.
>
> Invite your readers, pick your favorite genres, then press the golden book for a shelf—or spin the wheel and let fate choose.
>
> The winner becomes your club's current adventure.
>
> Here's what we really needed: a spoiler-safe reading room, with one conversation per chapter. Share theories, reactions, heart ratings, and voice notes without spoiling anyone reading behind.
>
> Thoughtful content warnings help everyone read comfortably, while progress check-ins keep the whole club together.
>
> No scattered chats. No endless debates. Just more reading, reacting, and book-club magic.
>
> Spines & Spins. Start your club free. Adventure is waiting.

## Voice direction

Read the opening as a personal confession, not a sales hook. Smile slightly on “I built it.” Pick up energy through the golden-book and wheel sequence. Slow down and sound most sincere on “what we really needed.” Give “No scattered chats. No endless debates.” two clean, confident beats. End warmly, with a small sense of wonder—not a hard sell.

The voiceover is about **126 words**. Read at roughly **130 words per minute**, leaving tiny pauses after “I built it,” after the wheel lands, and before the final brand line.

## Rotato polish rules

1. Use one phone model throughout; changing devices will make the walkthrough feel like separate ads.
2. Favor front and gentle three-quarter angles. Avoid steep perspective while showing detailed app screens.
3. Never rotate the device while viewers need to read. Animate into position, hold, then perform the screen action.
4. Keep camera motion slower than the screen interaction. The wheel is already the visual climax.
5. Use screen recordings for the wheel and scrolling; use still captures only for short hero holds.
6. Match every transition by shape or action: golden book glow → wheel center; selected cover → current-adventure cover; chapter row → discussion header.
7. Keep captions to two lines maximum and burn them in for sound-off viewing.
8. Use gold for discovery and CTAs, midnight green for reading, and burgundy only for content-care moments.
9. Do not show sign-in, setup friction, account settings, the membership screen, or the paywall in this hero ad.
10. Let the final logo frame breathe. Do not end on motion.

## Capture order

Record these clean app moments before building the Rotato project:

1. App icon or entrance screen
2. My Clubs / club card
3. Invite or member view
4. Genre shelf and golden-book tap
5. Chosen genre
6. Full book-wheel spin and result
7. Selected book detail
8. Current Adventure
9. Reading Room chapter list
10. Chapter Discussion with ratings and voice-note control
11. Content Care / trigger-warning flag
12. Reading Progress / reader check-ins

Capture each screen with one second of stillness before and after every interaction. That extra handle will make the Rotato timing and transitions much cleaner.
